WHO WE ARE

Quietly
exacting.

GHPL is a cybersecurity practice focused on authorized red team engagements, penetration testing, and purpose-built red team tooling.

Our job is not to create panic. It is to create evidence: what matters, what can be improved, and what to do next.

THE MISSION

Expose the
assumption.

Help organizations see how their systems, people, and processes hold up under credible pressure—then make the result useful.

THE VISION

Fewer
surprises.

A security culture where honest testing is routine, reporting is understood, and defensive decisions are made before an incident makes them urgent.

THE STANDARD

Core
values.

HOW WE WORK

A deliberate
sequence.

  1. 01

    Scoping

    Align objectives, systems, constraints, and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Rules of Engagement

    Document boundaries, contacts, timing, and escalation paths.

  3. 03

    Execution

    Conduct the authorized assessment with discipline and care.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    Translate evidence into prioritized findings and decisions.

  5. 05

    Debrief

    Walk through the result with the people responsible for acting on it.

  6. 06

    Retest

    Validate agreed remediation where a retest is in scope.

!

ETHICS + AUTHORIZATION

No signed scope.
No engagement.

We only test systems with signed written authorization and agreed rules of engagement. We respect boundaries, protect information, and stop when the scope says stop.
